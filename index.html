Fair questions about the newsletter

Is The Rent Roll free?

Yep. Free for Ontario landlords and property managers. Unsubscribe in one click from the bottom of any issue, no guilt trip.

Who is it for?

Anyone who owns or manages residential rental property in Ontario: one condo, a small portfolio, or a whole building. If the Residential Tenancies Act applies to you, so do we.

How often will you email me?

Once a week, plus a short heads-up when something urgent changes, like a new rent increase guideline or an LTB rule that moves a deadline.

Is this legal advice?

No. We explain Ontario tenancy law and Landlord and Tenant Board process in plain English so you can act sooner and ask smarter questions. For your specific situation, talk to a licensed paralegal or lawyer.

Do you cover cities outside Toronto?

Yes. Provincial rules apply everywhere, and we track municipal changes in Toronto, Ottawa, Hamilton, London, Kitchener-Waterloo, Windsor, Mississauga, Brampton and beyond.
