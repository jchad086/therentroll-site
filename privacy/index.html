The short version

  • We collect your email address, and whether you open and click our emails.
  • We use it to send you The Rent Roll and to figure out which stories land.
  • We have never sold your data and never will. No list rentals, no data brokers.
  • beehiiv sends the emails on our behalf and stores your address on servers in the United States.

  • Unsubscribe from any issue, or email us and we'll delete you entirely.

1. Who we are

The Rent Roll is a free weekly email newsletter for Ontario landlords and property managers. For the purposes of Canadian privacy law, we are the organization responsible for the personal information described on this page.

This policy is written to meet the requirements of the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). If you live in Quebec, Alberta or British Columbia, your provincial privacy law may give you additional rights, and we'll honour those too.

Canada's privacy framework is in the middle of a rewrite. In June 2026 the federal government tabled Bill C-36, which would replace part of PIPEDA with a new Protecting Privacy and Consumer Data Act. It is not law yet. If it passes, we'll update this page.

3. Why we collect it

  • To send you the newsletter. The obvious one.
  • To make it better. Open and click data tells us which stories are useful and which ones we should stop writing. We look at this in aggregate, not to study any one reader.
  • To keep the list clean. Removing addresses that bounce, and spotting bots and fake signups.
  • To prove consent. CASL requires us to be able to show when and how you subscribed.
  • To measure our own ads. When we advertise The Rent Roll on Facebook or Instagram, Meta's pixel tells us whether those ads led to visits and signups.
  • To report to advertisers. If we run a sponsorship, advertisers get aggregate numbers — total subscribers, open rate, click count. Never a list, never an individual address.

We do not build profiles of you, score you, sell your attention to data brokers, or use your personal information to train AI models.

4. Your consent

Entering your email on our site is express consent under CASL to receive The Rent Roll. Depending on our settings you may need to click a confirmation link before anything is sent, which gives both of us a clear record.

You can withdraw consent at any time, for any reason, using the unsubscribe link at the bottom of every issue. It works immediately and we won't email you to ask why. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything we lawfully did with your data before you withdrew it.

6. Where your data lives

beehiiv, Meta and some of our other providers store and process data on servers in the United States and elsewhere outside Canada. That means your email address leaves Canada.

While it's outside the country, it's subject to the laws of that jurisdiction, and foreign courts, law enforcement and national security authorities may be able to compel access to it under their own laws. This is true of essentially every email platform, but PIPEDA requires us to tell you plainly rather than bury it. If you're not comfortable with that, the honest answer is not to subscribe.

7. Cookies and tracking

On the website

This website uses Meta's pixel, a small piece of code from Facebook and Instagram's parent company. It sets cookies and tells Meta which pages you visit here and whether you subscribe. We use it to see whether our Facebook and Instagram ads lead to signups, and Meta may use it to decide who sees those ads. Meta may combine it with information it already holds about you. Your email address is never sent to Meta.

We run no other analytics or advertising trackers on this site. The signup form is embedded from beehiiv and may set its own cookies when you interact with it. A small beehiiv script on the homepage also passes along any campaign tags in the link you arrived on, such as which ad you clicked, so we can see which campaigns bring in subscribers.

To opt out, you can block third-party cookies or use a content blocker in your browser, change your ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings, or use the Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada's opt-out tools at youradchoices.ca.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work.

In the emails

Our emails contain a small tracking pixel that tells us whether an issue was opened, and links that record when they're clicked. This is standard newsletter practice and it's how we know what's worth writing.

If you'd rather not be counted, turn off image loading in your email client. That stops open tracking. Most clients let you do this per sender.

8. How long we keep it

  • While you're subscribed: for as long as you keep reading.
  • After you unsubscribe: we keep your email address on a suppression list so we don't accidentally email you again, and we keep a record of your original consent. CASL requires us to be able to prove consent for three years.
  • Engagement data: deleted or de-identified within 24 months.
  • Server and analytics logs: typically 30 to 90 days.
  • Meta pixel data: held by Meta under its own retention policy.
  • Emails you send us: as long as they're useful, then deleted.

If you ask for full deletion, we remove everything except the minimum record needed to honour your own unsubscribe request.

9. How we protect it

The site is served over HTTPS. Our beehiiv API credentials live in server-side environment variables and never appear in the website's code, so your address is never posted to a third party directly from your browser. Access to the subscriber list is limited to the people who run the newsletter and protected by strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication.

No system is perfectly secure, and we won't pretend otherwise. What we can promise is that we hold as little as possible, which is the only real protection.

10. Your rights

Under PIPEDA you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and what we've done with it.
  • Access a copy of it.
  • Correct it if it's wrong.
  • Withdraw consent and stop receiving the newsletter.
  • Delete it, subject to the small legal record described above.
  • Complain to us, and then to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

To exercise any of these, email privacy@therentroll.ca from the address you subscribed with. We respond within 30 days, usually much faster, and there's no charge. We may need to confirm it's really you before handing over data.

11. If something goes wrong

If there's a breach of security safeguards involving your personal information that creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will report it to the Privacy Commissioner and notify you as soon as feasible, as PIPEDA requires. We'll tell you what happened, what was affected, and what to do about it.

12. Children

The Rent Roll is written for adults who own or manage rental property. It's not directed at children and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has subscribed, email us and we'll remove them.

13. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change, and change the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle your personal information, we'll tell you in an issue before it takes effect rather than quietly editing the page.
